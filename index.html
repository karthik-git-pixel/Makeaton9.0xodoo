The hackathon

About Make-A-Ton

Welcome to Make-A-Ton 9.0: South India's Biggest Hackathon! The most exciting hackathon of the year is back with a bang. With a ₹1,05,000 prize pool up for grabs, we're set to make history.

Organized by CITTIC, Make-A-Ton is a coding marathon that fuels innovation. It starts with an 8-hour virtual round, and the shortlisted teams head to CUSAT, Kochi for a 24-hour final. Get ready for intense coding sessions, groundbreaking solutions and yes — plenty of coffee! Join us and leave your mark on the tech world!

Organized by

CITTIC

The Centre for Innovation, Technology Transfer and Industrial Collaboration (CITTIC), the technology business incubator of CUSAT, has nurtured several young startups and has supported the growth of technology in the student community.

CITTIC was set up on the campus with central and state government funding to provide a vibrant, innovative and entrepreneurial environment for faculty and students to run university-linked startup companies.

Our partner

About Odoo Hackathon

Odoo India’s hackathons are an open challenge to all tech enthusiasts and a great tool for Odoo to hire the best coders and designers to join Odoo India’s growing team.

Odoo India’s hiring hackathon journey began in June 2024 with Odoo Combat, its first open recruitment hackathon, attracting over 3,000 students and discovering exceptional young developers. Encouraged by its success, Odoo India collaborated with leading universities to bring hackathons closer to emerging talent, transforming a simple idea into a nationwide hackathon series.

Today, Odoo India has replaced traditional campus placements with hiring hackathons.

At Make-A-Ton 9.0, Odoo opens registrations, runs the 8-hour virtual round and announces the finalists.

Good to know

FAQ

What is Make-A-Ton 9.0?

The ninth edition of Make-A-Ton, the hackathon run by CITTIC at CUSAT, this year with Odoo.

How does it work?

There are two rounds. The 8-hour virtual round is on 31 October 2026, with results on 19 November 2026. Shortlisted teams move on to the 24-hour final round on 19–20 December 2026.

When can I register?

Registrations open on 21 September 2026 and close on 25 October 2026.

Where is the final round?

At CUSAT, Kochi.

What can we win?

₹45,000 for the winning team, ₹35,000 for the first runner-up and ₹25,000 for the second runner-up.

Who can take part, and how big can teams be?

Eligibility and team-size details will be shared when registrations open.

Could the dates change?

The schedule may be updated. Any changes will be posted on this page.
