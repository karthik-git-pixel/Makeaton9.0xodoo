About Make-A-Ton

Every developer’s journey has a defining chapter — the moment an idea becomes reality and potential meets opportunity. For many, that project began at Make-A-Ton.

Over the past eight editions, thousands of students have come together to build, experiment, fail, rebuild and grow. Along the way came first hackathons, first prototypes, first internships, first startup ideas, and first opportunities that extended far beyond the event itself.

This year, the story takes a new turn.

In collaboration with Odoo, Make-A-Ton 9.0 returns as South India’s largest hiring hackathon — bringing your work in front of the people looking for the next generation of builders. Because sometimes, the project you build over a weekend becomes the one that opens the door to your first career opportunity.

CITTIC

It begins at CITTIC.

The Centre for Innovation, Technology Transfer and Industrial Collaboration (CITTIC) has spent years fostering innovation, supporting student-led startups, and creating opportunities for young builders to collaborate with industry.

The same ecosystem that has helped ideas grow into ventures continues to shape Make-A-Ton — bringing together talent, mentorship and opportunity under one roof.

About Odoo Hackathon

You’ve got the skills. You just haven’t had the right chance to show them.

Odoo’s hiring hackathons are designed around a simple idea: the best way to understand a developer is to see them build. How they think. How they solve. How they work with others. What they do when the first idea doesn’t work.

This year, Odoo × Make-A-Ton 9.0 brings that experience to you — opening a direct path from building to being noticed, and from being noticed to your next opportunity.

FAQ

How do I register for Make-A-Ton 9.0?

Registrations open on 21 September 2026 and close on 25 October 2026. The registration link will be posted here and announced on our channels.

Who is eligible to participate in Make-A-Ton 9.0?

Eligibility details will be shared when registrations open.

What is HackForCUSAT?

HackForCUSAT is a 12-hour hackathon exclusively for CUSAT students and serves as the curtain-raiser for Make-A-Ton 9.0. It runs on 18 December 2026.

Do I need previous hackathon experience?

No. HackForCUSAT in particular is built for first-time hackers — your first team, your first build, your first hackathon.

Is there any registration fee?

Fee details will be shared when registrations open.

What is the team size?

Team-size rules will be shared when registrations open.

How does the Odoo hiring opportunity work?

Odoo uses the hackathon to see how you build rather than how you interview. Top performers get the opportunity to be considered for hiring at Odoo.

Will participating guarantee a job at Odoo?

No. Taking part puts your work in front of Odoo and makes top performers eligible to be considered for hiring, but it is not a guarantee of a role.

How can I get in touch with the Make-A-Ton team?
