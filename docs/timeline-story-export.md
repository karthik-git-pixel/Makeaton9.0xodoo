## 1. Overview & Narrative Arc

The Make-A-Ton hackathon timeline is structured as a **5-part indie graphic novel narrative**. The story tracks a builder squad from the initial spark of registration, through high-stakes midnight submissions, an intense 8-hour virtual elimination hack, the triumph of the Kochi shortlist, and culminating in a 24-hour on-campus grand finale at CUSAT.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 MAKE-A-TON RUN SHEET CHRONOLOGY                                   |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  CHAPTER I            CHAPTER II           CHAPTER III          CHAPTER IV          CHAPTER V     |
|  21 Sep 2026          10 Nov 2026          14 Nov 2026          26 Nov 2026         19-20 Dec 2026|
|  Registration Opens   Registration Ends    Virtual Round        Round Results       Grand Finale  |
|  [Odoo Portal]        [CUSAT Initiative]   [Hosted by Odoo]     [Shortlist]         [On-Site CUSAT|
|  "The Form is Live!"  "11:59 PM Submit!"   "8 Hrs on Clock!"    "Going to Kochi!"   "24h Shipping"|
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## 5. Summary & Mapping Guide

* **5 Chapters / 5 Milestones:** Spanning 21 Sep $\to$ 20 Dec.
* **Dual-Owner Structure:** Odoo (Portal, Virtual Hack, Judging) $\leftrightarrow$ CUSAT (Registrations, Campus Hosting, Stage).
* **Multi-Layer Comic Graphic Novel Aesthetics:**
  1. Background artwork (`.png` panels).
  2. Top-left yellow monospace timestamp narration boxes (`#FFDE59`).
  3. Tail-pointed dialogue speech bubbles with bold punchline highlights (`#FFE600`).
  4. Slanted brand & milestone authentication badges (`-3deg` to `+2deg`).
